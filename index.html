Hermes rclone

A private, single-user file-transfer utility.

What it does

Hermes rclone is a personal command-line utility built on rclone. Its only function is to move files between a single person's own computer and that same person's own Google Drive — downloading a file the owner has uploaded, and uploading a result back to the owner's Drive.

Who uses it

Exactly one person: the owner of the Google account that authorises it. The utility is not distributed, not published in any app store, and not offered to other users. There is no sign-up, no account system and no server-side component owned by the author.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive files belonging to the account that grants access, via the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope.
  • Nothing else: no Gmail, no Calendar, no contacts, no Google account profile data.

Data handling

Files are transferred directly between the owner's computer and the owner's Drive. The author runs no servers, collects no analytics, and receives no copies of any file. Authorisation tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine. Full detail on the privacy policy page.
